⚡ Dual-Stream Architectural Pipeline
STAGE 01
Dual Ingestion
PyMuPDF extracts raw text while rendering pages into 2.0x high-res PNG images.
STAGE 02
Multi-Vector Qdrant
Dense text vectors (BGE) indexed with full Base64 page images stored inside Point payload.
STAGE 03
Multimodal Retrieval
Top-k query search retrieves matching text chunk + its original high-fidelity page image.
STAGE 04
Vision LLM Reasoning
Gemini Flash Vision consumes both text and page image, answering with 100% spatial grounding.
📊 Engineering Trade-Off Matrix
Architectural Dimension Traditional Text-Only RAG Dual-Stream Multimodal Vision RAG
Spatial Layout & Multi-Column Collapsed into 1D text stream; structure destroyed 100% preserved through high-res page rendering
Financial & Hierarchical Tables Headers decouple from cell values; frequent drift Model visually cross-references headers and row grid
Charts, Graphs & Trendlines Discarded completely or reduced to noise tokens Reads legends, colors, bar heights, and numeric labels
Hallucination Mitigation High risk when queries involve visual document assets Near zero; answers are anchored directly to visual pixels
Storage & Compute Footprint Low (vector only) Optimized (text vector + compressed PNG payload in Qdrant)
